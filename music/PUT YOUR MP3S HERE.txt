Drop your downloaded MP3s / WAVs here.

In the app: open the player (bottom-right pill ▲) → "My music" → "Pick my music folder"
→ choose this folder → every track appears. Tap a track to play; it keeps playing until
you stop it, and it loops to the next one automatically.

Tip: the built-in "Sounds" (Deep Space, Brown Noise, Pink Rain, brainwave tones) need
no files at all — try those first.
